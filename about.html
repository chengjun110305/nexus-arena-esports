Who we are

One campus. Many ways to play.

Founded in 2022 by a small group of students looking for reliable teammates, Nexus Arena has grown into a club for competitive squads, casual players, stream producers and esports event volunteers.

We organise structured tournaments without losing the social side of gaming. Members can practise seriously, learn a new title, help run a broadcast or simply join a relaxed Friday lobby.

Weekly life

What happens at Nexus Arena

Our programme balances competition, learning and low-pressure social play.

Every Tuesday

Team practice & VOD review

Division captains run structured scrims and review decision-making together.

Every Friday

Open game night

Walk-in lobbies, console stations and beginner-friendly matchmaking in the club room.

Once a month

Community cup

Short-form competitions with mixed teams, fair play awards and spectator activities.
